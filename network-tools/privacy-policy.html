Network Tools — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

At KARELab ("we", "us", "our"), we care about the privacy of users of the Network Tools mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and what your rights are.

1. Information We Collect

The following information may be collected automatically when you use the App:

  • Your device's IP address
  • Screens visited, along with the date and time of each visit
  • Time spent in the App and frequency of use
  • Mobile operating system and device information (brand, model, OS version)
  • Data collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies (SDKs, pixels)
  • If you subscribe to Network Tools Pro: an anonymous app user ID and purchase information (see Section 4)

Since the App provides network diagnostic tools (ping, port scanning, DNS lookup, etc.), it may access the network information required to perform these functions (your local IP, information about the network you are connected to). This information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to us; only the analytics/diagnostic data described above is shared with third-party services.

2. How We Use This Information

  • To operate and improve the App
  • To perform debugging and crash analysis
  • To analyze usage statistics in order to improve the user experience
  • To display ads and measure ad performance
  • To verify and activate your Network Tools Pro subscription

4. Subscriptions (Network Tools Pro)

If you choose to subscribe to Network Tools Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play; your payment details (card number, etc.) are never received by us or by RevenueCat. We use RevenueCat to verify the validity of your subscription; RevenueCat only receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information (product, date, status) — it does not receive your name or payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through Google Play.

5. Data Retention

  • User-provided data: For as long as you use the App, plus 12 months
  • Automatically collected data: Up to 24 months
  • Aggregated/anonymized data: May be retained indefinitely (not considered personal data)

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. You can contact us regarding your requests at kareller.ek@gmail.com.

For California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion of that information, and to opt out of the sale of your personal information. The App does not sell your personal information for money.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from users in this age group. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it from our systems.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly. For significant changes, a notice may be shown within the App.
