Privacy Policy — DNS Changer

Last updated: 2026-08-10

Overview

DNS Changer ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android utility app that lets you choose which DNS resolver your device uses. The app does not require an account — there is no sign-in, and no per-device limit. The app is free to use, with an optional Pro subscription (see below). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's used.

What we collect

  • DNS server selection: which DNS server (from our list, or one you added yourself) you've selected. We do not log or inspect the content of your DNS queries or browsing activity.
  • Custom DNS servers you add (Pro feature): names and IP addresses of any custom DNS entries you create are stored locally on your device only. We do not receive or store them on our servers.
  • Usage analytics: aggregate, non-identifying events (via Firebase Analytics) used to understand feature usage.
  • Advertising data (Free plan only): the app shows ads via Google AdMob to users on the Free plan. AdMob may use the advertising ID and other identifiers to serve and measure ads, subject to the consent choices you make in the in-app privacy/consent prompt (Google's User Messaging Platform). See Google's own privacy policy for details on AdMob data handling.
  • Subscription status (Pro plan): if you subscribe to Pro, your purchase is processed entirely by Google Play — we never see or store your payment details. To confirm and manage your entitlement, subscription status is shared with our subscription infrastructure provider, RevenueCat, which receives a device/account identifier and purchase status from Google Play. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect an account, email address, or any sign-in information — the app has no login of its own (Pro entitlement is tied to your Google Play account, managed by Google Play and RevenueCat).
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not inspect, log, or store the contents of your DNS queries or general network traffic.
  • We do not track or limit which or how many devices you use the app on.
  • We do not show ads to Pro subscribers.

How the DNS feature works

When you select a DNS server, the app changes your device's DNS resolver to the one you chose, using Android's VpnService API to intercept only DNS traffic (port 53). This is limited to DNS resolution — the app does not route, inspect, or proxy your general internet traffic. If you've enabled the DNS changer and your device restarts, the app may restart the service so your chosen DNS setting continues to apply.

Pro subscription

Pro is an optional, auto-renewing subscription (monthly or annual) that removes ads and lets you add an unlimited number of custom DNS servers. It is billed and managed entirely through Google Play. You can view, manage, or cancel your subscription at any time from your Google Play account settings. See our Terms of Service for full subscription terms.

Your choices

  • You can change or revoke your ad consent/personalization choices at any time from the Privacy Policy option in the app's settings.
  • You can stop the DNS changer service at any time from within the app.
  • You can manage or cancel your Pro subscription at any time from your Google Play account settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes all local app data from your device.
