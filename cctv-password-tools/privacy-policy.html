01 Overview

CCTV Password Tools ("the app") is a utility that calculates manufacturer default and time-based reset passwords for DVR/NVR devices (Hikvision, Dahua, Xiongmai/XM, TVT, RaySharp, iCatch, KBVision, Streamax, Fuho, and related OEM brands), based on the date you enter and the country you select.

All password calculations are performed locally on your device. The app does not ask you to create an account, does not require you to enter any camera or DVR login credentials, and does not transmit the dates, device models, or results you calculate to the developer or to any server we control.

The app does include third-party advertising and analytics libraries, described below, which operate independently of the app's core functionality and collect data under their own privacy terms.

02 Information the app itself does not collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any account/registration data — there is no account system.
  • DVR/NVR passwords, usernames, IP addresses of your cameras, or any input you type into the calculator screens.
  • Photos, videos, or files from your device.
  • Precise (GPS) location.

03 Information collected by third-party services

The app is funded by advertising and uses the following Google services, which may collect data as described in their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob (ads)

Displays banner, interstitial, and native ads inside the app. AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (AD_ID), IP address (used to derive an approximate region), device and app information, and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland, ad personalization is subject to the consent choices described below.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP / consent management)

Used to show a consent form to users in regions covered by GDPR/UK data protection law and to store your consent choice (e.g. personalized vs. non-personalized ads) locally and with Google, so the app can respect it on future launches.

Firebase Analytics

Collects usage analytics such as screens viewed, app opens, general device/app information (device model, OS version, language, approximate region), and app diagnostics, to help us understand feature usage and improve the app. This data is associated with an app-instance identifier, not with your name or contact details.

These providers act as independent data controllers for the data they collect. Review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data when you use AdMob partner sites or apps for details on what each service collects and how to control it.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of the app, its developer, or users.

06 Your choices & rights

  • Ad personalization: if you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you can change your consent choice at any time from the consent dialog shown by the app; elsewhere, you can limit ad tracking from your device's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Reset advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID (or the equivalent on your Android version).
  • Uninstalling the app stops all further data collection by the app and its embedded SDKs going forward.
  • GDPR (EEA/UK): you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data held by Google's services via Google's own account controls, since the app itself retains no server-side personal data of its own.
  • CCPA (California): we do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.

07 Children's privacy

The app is a technical utility intended for installers and technicians and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app's third-party services, please contact us so we can address it.

08 Data retention & security

Because the app does not operate its own backend, it retains no server-side copy of your activity. Data collected by Google's AdMob, UMP, and Firebase Analytics is retained according to Google's own retention schedules, described in the policies linked in Section 3.
