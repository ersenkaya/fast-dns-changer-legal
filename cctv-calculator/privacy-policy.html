CCTV Calculator and Tools — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

KARELab ("we", "us", "our") cares about the privacy of users of the CCTV Calculator and Tools mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and what your rights are.

1. Information We Collect

The following information may be automatically collected when you use the App:

  • Your device's IP address
  • Screens visited, and the date and time of visits
  • Time spent in the App and usage frequency
  • Mobile operating system and device information (brand, model, OS version)
  • Data collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies (SDKs, pixels)

Location Permission

The App requests Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) permission to display the name (SSID) and access point address (BSSID) of the WiFi network you are connected to; this information is shared by the Android operating system only when location permission is granted. This permission is not used to determine or record your device's geographic location, and is never stored or transmitted to us or to any third party; it is only read momentarily on your device to be shown on screen at that instant.

Network Diagnostics and Scanning Features

The App provides network diagnostic and CCTV installation tools (ping, port scanning, local network device discovery, IP/DNS information, WiFi signal information, etc.). To perform these functions, it may access the network information required (your local IP address, information about the network you are connected to, and the IP address - and network name, if available - of devices on the same local network). This data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us or stored on our servers.

CCTV Calculation Tools

Values you enter into calculation tools such as storage, bandwidth, lens, viewing angle, RAID, and power budget are processed only on your device and are never sent to or stored by us.

Subscription and Purchase Information

If you purchase a Premium subscription, purchase and subscription status data (such as your selected plan, purchase date, and renewal status) is processed through Google Play Billing and our subscription management provider, RevenueCat. RevenueCat assigns an anonymous app user identifier to your installation to manage your subscription across sessions and devices. Your payment details (such as your card number) are handled entirely by Google Play and are never seen, transmitted to, or stored by us or by RevenueCat.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve the App
  • To perform debugging and crash analysis
  • To analyze usage statistics to improve the user experience
  • To display ads and measure ad performance
  • To manage and verify Premium subscriptions

4. Data Retention Period

  • User-provided data: For as long as you use the App, plus 12 months
  • Automatically collected data: Up to 24 months
  • Aggregated/anonymized data: May be retained indefinitely (does not constitute personal data)
  • Subscription and purchase data: Retained for as long as your subscription is active, plus any additional period required for accounting, tax, or legal purposes, in line with Google Play's and RevenueCat's own retention policies

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data. You can contact us at kareller.ek@gmail.com to submit such requests.

For California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, and to opt out of the sale of your personal information. The App does not sell your personal information for monetary value.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from users in this age group. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it from our systems.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly. For material changes, a notice may be shown within the App.
